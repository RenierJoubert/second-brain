# Monitoring GDP

Because GDP is painstakingly calculated across hundreds of data sources it takes a month to come out after a quarter finishes, so it is not terribly helpful to investors and is a lagging indicator. So, we can use more frequent and timely indicators that typically correlate with GDP to make our guess.

## The World Economic Calendar (WECO)

1. PMI Business Confidence Indicator (first business day of following month)
2. Change in Nonfarm payrolls (first Friday of the following month)
3. Housing starts (middle of the following month)
4. CPI (middle of the following month)
5. GDP (released a month after the end of the quarter)
