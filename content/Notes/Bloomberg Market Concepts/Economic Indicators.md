# The 5 Key Economic Indicators

>1. Economic Growth
>2. Inflation
>3. Unemployment
>4. Business Confidence
>5. Housing

## Economic Growth

GDP (gross domestic product), is the market value of all G&S (goods and services) produced in a country, the broadest most comprehensive barometer of economic growth provided by the formula:

$$
GDP = C + I + G + (X-M)
$$
>C = personal consumption
>I = private investment
>G = government consumption
>X = Exports
>M = Imports

### Nominal vs Real GDP

Nominal growth in GDP (measured in dollars) is not an accurate measure of GDP as it does not account for inflation. This is why we opt to rather use real GDP provided by:
$$
\text{Real GDP Growth(\%)} = \text{Nominal GDP Growth(\%)} + \text{Inflation(\%)}
$$

### Recession

A recession is defined by two-successive quarters of negative GDP growth.

## Inflation

Inflation is the general increase in prices of goods and services that diminishes the purchase power of money. 

### Sources of Inflation Data

>1. PCE Report (Monthly personal consumption expenditures)
>2. CPI (Consumer price index)

For the CPI to be truly representative it needs to reflect spending habits in the associated country (what goods are in the measured basket of goods).

## Unemployment

Consumers make up more than 2/3 of the U.S economy, and this spending is purely driven by salaries. An overall increase in unemployment heavily depresses economic growth and is also critical political statistic.

### Nonfarm payrolls

A statistic that measures the monthly change in the number of employees in the U.S, excluding seasonal agricultural workers.

## Business Confidence

The PMI (purchasing managers index) is also a leading indicator of GDP growth.

## Housing

House building only accounts for 3% of the U.S economy but is still a leading economic indicator as before homes are built they need to believe that consumers are confident to assume mortgages, moreover, consumers after buying a home need to furnish it, landscape it, buy services, etc. Thus creating much more economic stimulus than that initial 3%/
