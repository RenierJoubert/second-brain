Currency arbitrage - Price didn't always move quickly enough to reflect changes in currency pairs which allowed for arbitrage. This is now not possible except in cases of extreme volatility. 

In theory currency should adjust in value against each other so a basket of goods costs the same when in priced in one currency vs another (PPP). But sometimes currencies can remain under or over valued for long periods of time so the PPP is not useful for making predictions. 

## Primary Methods for Analyzing Currency Movement
- Macroeconomics
- Sentiment analysis

### Macro
We assume all information is available to all investors simultaneously. all investors are rationale and seek to maximize their reward and minimize their risk.

Factors used in macroeconomc analysis tend to interact with eachother rather than have an isolated impact. 

### Sentiment
As humans investors are incapable of maintaining maximum efficiency and rationality creating behavioral patterns that can be forecast.


## Economic Indicators
As a countries currency becomes more inflationary, investors holding that currency will sell it as its capacity to purchase goods and services will diminish. investors as a result try to anticipate inflation. 

Interest rates are linked with inflation and growtth, but also have an impact on currency flows. central banks raise rates when inflation is high, and vice versa when inflation is low.

The yield differential is the difference in interest rates of two currencies. This is closely watched. 

Bond yield differential

CPI and PPI drive short term inflation rates. 

## Summary
In the short run the three main drivers of currency valuation is:
1. Surprise changes in interest rates.
2. Surprise changed in inflation.
3. Surprise changes in trade.
